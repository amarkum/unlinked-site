Something not working, or have an idea? Email hello@stackcone.com and include your Chrome version and what you saw on the LinkedIn feed.

Common questions

Posts are not being hidden. Reload the LinkedIn tab, then check the filter you picked in the extension popup.

How do I see a hidden post? Click the thin "N posts hidden" line to bring them back.

Does it collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
