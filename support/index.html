Common questions

Posts are not being hidden

Reload the LinkedIn tab, make sure the switch at the top of the Unlinked popup is on, and check that you are on www.linkedin.com. Then check which filter is selected.

Unlinked hides too much

Switch to the lighter Ads & suggestions filter, or choose Custom and turn off the items you want to keep.

How do I see a hidden post?

Click the thin “N posts hidden” line where the posts were, or turn Unlinked off from the popup.

How do I start over?

Open the popup and press Reset to go back to the default settings.

LinkedIn changed and something slipped through

LinkedIn updates its site often. If Unlinked stops hiding something, email a screenshot (with any personal details covered) and it will be fixed.

What to include in a bug report

  • Your Chrome version and operating system
  • The filter you are using
  • What you expected and what you saw
