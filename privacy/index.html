Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Unlinked is a Chrome extension that hides unwanted posts in the LinkedIn home feed. It is designed to collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. Unlinked does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or page content. It has no analytics, no tracking and no servers.

How it works

The extension runs only on linkedin.com. It reads the feed in your browser to decide which posts to hide, and all of that happens on your device.

What is stored

  • Your settings (chosen filter, switches, muted keywords and muted people) are saved with Chrome's storage and synced through your own Chrome sync account, if you have it turned on.
  • Recently seen author names, used for muted-people suggestions, stay on your device.

You can remove all of this by uninstalling the extension or clearing its data.

Third parties

Unlinked sends no data to any third party and loads no remote code.
