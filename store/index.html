Get Unlinked for Chrome

Unlinked - Clean LinkedIn Feed · Free Chrome extension

Coming soon to the Chrome Web StoreHow it works

Unlinked cleans up your LinkedIn home feed so you see posts from the people and pages you follow, without ads, suggestions and other filler.

What you get

  • Filters you can pick in one click: Ads & suggestions, Recommended, Aggressive or your own Custom mix
  • Hides promoted posts, suggested posts, “liked by” and “commented on” activity, and posts from people you don’t follow
  • Switches for reposts, celebrations, hiring posts, polls, videos, sidebars, the messaging pop-up and like and comment counts
  • Muted keywords and muted people, with name suggestions as you type
  • Focus mode, and a thin “N posts hidden” line that brings removed posts back

Permissions, explained

  • storage: saves your filter choice, switches, muted keywords and muted people.
  • Access to www.linkedin.com: lets Unlinked read the feed on the page so it can hide posts. It runs nowhere else.

Unlinked asks for nothing else, uses no remote code and sends no data anywhere.

Private by design. No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Read the privacy policy, get help on the support page, or see the guide to cleaning up your LinkedIn feed.
