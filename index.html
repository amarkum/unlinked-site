Unlinked - Clean LinkedIn Feed

See posts from the people and pages you follow, without promoted, suggested and other noise.

Pick a filter

  • Ads & suggestions: hides promoted posts and LinkedIn's suggestions
  • Recommended: also hides posts from people you don't follow, and posts shown because someone in your network liked or commented on them
  • Aggressive: hides everything except posts from people you follow, plus the clutter around the feed
  • Custom: your own mix of switches, remembered for next time

Advanced options hide reposts, celebrations, hiring posts, polls, videos, sidebars, the messaging pop-up, like and comment counts, and posts matching muted keywords or people. Focus mode hides the whole feed when you only want to post or check messages.

Unlinked runs only on linkedin.com, works entirely in your browser and sends no data anywhere.
