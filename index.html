Pick a LinkedIn feed filter in one click

Four filters cover most feeds. Change them any time from the toolbar popup.

Ads & suggestionsHides promoted posts and LinkedIn’s suggestions.
RecommendedAlso hides posts from people you don’t follow, and posts shown because someone in your network liked or commented on them.
AggressiveHides everything except posts from people you follow, plus the clutter around the feed.
CustomYour own mix of switches, remembered for next time.

Fine-tune exactly what disappears

Open Advanced to turn individual filters on or off and tidy up the rest of the page.

  • Hide reposts, celebrations, hiring posts, polls and videos
  • Hide the news and puzzles sidebar, your profile sidebar, the messaging pop-up, and like and comment counts
  • Mute keywords and people, with name suggestions as you type
  • Use focus mode to hide the whole feed when you only want to post or check messages
  • Click the thin “N posts hidden” line to bring removed posts back

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about cleaning up your LinkedIn feed with Unlinked.

What is Unlinked?

Unlinked is a free Chrome extension that cleans up your LinkedIn home feed. It hides promoted posts, suggested posts, “liked by” and “commented on” activity and other filler, so the feed shows posts from the people and pages you follow.

How do I clean up my LinkedIn feed?

Install Unlinked, open linkedin.com and pick a filter from the toolbar popup. Recommended keeps posts from people you follow, Ads & suggestions is lighter, and Aggressive hides everything else. Under Advanced you can switch individual items on or off. Read the full guide to cleaning up your LinkedIn feed.

Can I hide LinkedIn ads and promoted posts?

Yes. The Ads & suggestions filter hides promoted posts and LinkedIn’s suggestions, and you can switch ads and suggestions off separately under Advanced.

Can I still see the posts Unlinked hides?

Yes. Removed posts leave a thin “N posts hidden” line that you can click to bring them back, and you can turn Unlinked off at any time with the switch at the top of the popup.

Does Unlinked collect my data?

No. Unlinked runs only on linkedin.com, works entirely in your browser and sends no data anywhere. Your settings are saved with Chrome sync, and recently seen author names used for muted-people suggestions stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

Which browsers does it work in?

Unlinked is built for Chrome on desktop. Other Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Brave can usually install Chrome Web Store extensions, but Chrome is the supported browser.

Is Unlinked free?

Yes, it is free to use. If you find it useful you can support the project through GitHub Sponsors.

Is Unlinked affiliated with LinkedIn?

No. Unlinked is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
